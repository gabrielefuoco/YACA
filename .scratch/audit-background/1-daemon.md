## 2. FRAGILITY (Top 10 Most Serious)

1. **DuckDB `2GB` memory limit inside a `1024m` container (OOM kill risk)**
   - Code: `src/indice/duckDbStore.js:226` passes `{ max_memory: '2GB' }` to `new duckdb.Database(...)`, while `docker-compose.yml:139` caps `yaca-tmdb-daemon` at `mem_limit: 1024m` (and `yaca-app` at `1536m`, `:58`).
   - Blast radius: `rebuildAndSwap()`, `convert_to_parquet.js`, `buildTier2()`, or `buildPeopleIndex()` can exceed 1024 MB and trigger a Linux `SIGKILL` OOM kill on `yaca-tmdb-daemon`, aborting sync mid-write and leaving `.tmp`/`.new`/`.draining` files on `/data/tmdb`.

2. **Double back-to-back rebuilds in `dailySync` + unguarded concurrent background rebuilds in `coldStart`**
   - Code: `services/tmdb-daemon/tmdbDumpDaemon.js:407-414` runs `await convertAndReloadDuckDb(store)` and `:499-502` immediately runs it a second time. Worse, during `coldStart`, `:252-256` fires `convertAndReloadDuckDb(store).catch(...)` unawaited every 5,000 items with no mutex — overlapping runs share fixed temp paths (`movies_tmp.parquet`, `catalog.duckdb.new`, `titles_fts5.db.tmp`, `people.db.tmp`).
   - Blast radius: Concurrent `convert_to_parquet.js` / `rebuildAndSwap` / `buildTier2` / `buildPeopleIndex` invocations race on temp files (`ENOENT`/`EBUSY` failures) and can OOM the 1 GB daemon container.

3. **Silent failure masking in `convertAndReloadDuckDb`, `buildTier2Index`, and `buildPeopleIndexFile` + false-healthy status**
   - Code: `services/tmdb-daemon/tmdbDumpDaemon.js:81-106,108-134` catch all errors in `buildTier2Index`, `buildPeopleIndexFile`, and `convertAndReloadDuckDb` without rethrowing; `:504-509` then sets `phase: 'idle'` and updates `lastSync` to `now`. `cli.js:29` healthcheck only fails on `s.isStale || s.phase === 'error'`.
   - Blast radius: Parquet conversion, DuckDB swap, Tier 2 FTS5 build, or `people.db` build can fail completely while `--health-check` and `dump_status.json` report healthy/idle (`OK`) and `yaca-app` keeps serving stale/broken indices.

4. **`yaca-app` opens `catalog.duckdb` read-write and runs `CHECKPOINT` + `init()` can unlink `catalog.duckdb`**
   - Code: `src/indice/duckDbStore.js:226` opens `catalog.duckdb` without `access_mode: 'READ_ONLY'`, and `repopulatePostReopen` (`:454-469`) calls `ensureItaColumn()` (`:769-790`), which executes `ALTER TABLE ...` + `CHECKPOINT;` on the live DB after every reopen. Meanwhile `swapDatabase` (`:99-103`) deletes `${toPath}.wal` before rename, and `init()` (`:555-557`) calls `_cleanupDbFiles(this.dbPath)` (deleting `catalog.duckdb`) on any non-lock open failure.
   - Blast radius: Concurrent WAL writes / lock contention during daemon swap or `yaca-app` restart can corrupt/delete `catalog.duckdb`, dropping `yaca-app` into empty fallback tables (`:592,600`) and serving empty catalogs.

5. **Silent data loss in `TmdbDumpStore.upsert` spool draining & unbuffered `_processFileSafely` stream**
   - Code: `services/tmdb-daemon/tmdbDumpStore.js:128-157,194-198` wraps `renameSync(spoolPath, claimed)` and `readClaimed(claimed)` in silent `try {} catch (_) {}`, silently ignores `JSON.parse` failures in `readClaimed`, and unconditionally `unlinkSync(claimed)` at `:195`. Also `_processFileSafely` (`:89-121`) ignores `writeStream.write()` backpressure and does not attach the `error` listener before the `for await` write loop.
   - Blast radius: Any I/O error or malformed line in `promoted_*.jsonl` permanently deletes promoted Tier 1 records; unthrottled writes on large `master_movies.jsonl` buffer into RAM.

6. **Unbounded disk growth of `/data/tmdb/export/*.json.gz` + duplicate appends on `coldStart` crash**
   - Code: `src/indice/tier2.js:109-131` caches `movie_ids_MM_DD_YYYY.json.gz` and `tv_series_ids_MM_DD_YYYY.json.gz` in `<dataDir>/export/` (~25-35 MB/day) with zero pruning/rotation. Meanwhile `tmdbDumpDaemon.js:235-245` calls `store.appendBatch()` before `store.saveCursor()` with no file lock or dedup.
   - Blast radius: `/data/tmdb/export/` grows indefinitely (~10+ GB/year) until `/data/tmdb` runs out of space, breaking Parquet/DuckDB/SQLite swaps; crashing between `appendBatch` and `saveCursor` leaves duplicate rows in `master_*.jsonl`.

7. **Immediate `process.exit(0)` on `SIGTERM`/`SIGINT` interrupts active writes/swaps**
   - Code: `services/tmdb-daemon/cli.js:37-46` calls `stopDaemon()` and immediately calls `process.exit(0)` without awaiting in-flight `upsert`, `convert_to_parquet.js`, `rebuildAndSwap()`, or `buildTier2()`.
   - Blast radius: Every Watchtower deploy or container stop while a sync/build is running kills the process mid-operation, leaving stale `.tmp`/`.new`/`.draining` files and an out-of-date `cursor.json`.

8. **`Tier2SearchClient` and `PeopleSearchClient` never reopen SQLite after daemon swaps `titles_fts5.db` / `people.db`**
   - Code: `src/indice/tier2.js:445-449` and `src/indice/people.js:348-354` cache `this.db` once (`if (this.db) return true;`) with no file-identity (`mtimeMs`/`size`) watchdog, while `buildTier2` (`tier2.js:402`) and `buildPeopleIndex` (`people.js:302`) replace the files via `fs.renameSync`.
   - Blast radius: On Linux, `yaca-app` keeps querying the unlinked old inode until container restart, missing all newly promoted/synced Tier 2 titles and people and holding deleted disk blocks open.

9. **Non-atomic first-time Parquet write in `convert_to_parquet.js`**
   - Code: `scripts/convert_to_parquet.js:204-205,229` sets `writerFile = destinationExisted ? tmpParquetFile : parquetFile`, writing directly to `movies.parquet` / `tv.parquet` on initial creation.
   - Blast radius: If `yaca-app` starts or `buildPeopleIndex()` runs while initial Parquet conversion is incomplete or crashes, DuckDB reads a truncated/corrupt `.parquet` file.

10. **Brittle regex ID extraction in `TmdbDumpStore.loadIds` + silent line drops across JSONL readers**
    - Code: `services/tmdb-daemon/tmdbDumpStore.js:57-64` parses IDs using `/"id":\s*(\d+)/` instead of `JSON.parse` (even though `buildMovieRow`/`buildTvRow` spread `...common` *before* `id: data.id` at `tmdbDumpClient.js:143-144`), and `_processFileSafely` (`104-106`), `filterDailyExportStream` (`tmdbDumpClient.js:442`), and `convert_to_parquet.js:103` (`ignore_errors=true`) drop malformed lines without logging or counting.
    - Blast radius: Any unescaped nested `"id":` before top-level `id` causes `loadIds` to track the wrong ID (skipping updates or deleting wrong items in `dailySync`); corrupt lines in `master_*.jsonl` vanish silently.
